## 1. Our Commitment to Transparency

Free Git Hosting is a blog dedicated to educating developers, students, and technology enthusiasts about **Git hosting**, **version control**, and **repository management**.  
Some of our posts may contain **affiliate links** — meaning we may earn a small commission if you click on those links or make a purchase, at **no extra cost to you**.

These commissions help us maintain our website, write detailed tutorials, and continue sharing useful content about **GitHub hosting** and related tools.

---

## 2. What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are unique URLs provided by companies (such as hosting providers or Git-related platforms) that track referrals from our website.  
When you click on such a link and make a purchase, the company rewards us with a small referral commission.

For example:
- A link to a Git hosting provider’s premium plan.
- A recommendation for a domain or static site deployment tool for GitHub Pages.
- A tutorial mentioning integrations or services related to Git hosting.

All affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly in the relevant blog posts.

## 3. Our Editorial Integrity

Even though we may receive commissions from affiliate programs, we **never compromise our editorial standards**.

We prioritize providing value and reliability over promotional intent.  
Each recommendation aligns with our mission — to help developers and creators understand and maximize the potential of GitHub hosting and related services.
